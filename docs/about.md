# Welcome to the ADHD task manager project

In this repo I will be attempting to tackle providing those with ADHD a free open-source way to tackle their daily tasks whether at work or in their daily lives. This was inspired by my 12 year old son who like me has ADHD and utilizing a calendar app where he has to manage his own tasks is a struggle. As a functioning adult with ADHD I too find it hard to always keep my calendar managed but I have years of practice ahead of my son. So my hopes are to help him struggle less in life so he can focuss more on living it.

## Initial planning phase

Current plan is to build a tool for CLI and then an API so that this can be utilized by anyone and everyone and then ultimately serve up our own website. If possible I want to make this as an app as well so that my son can use it on his phone and maybe eventually smart watch functionality.
